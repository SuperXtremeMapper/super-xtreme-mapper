USB/MIDI
If you have a PC/Mac with MIDI software installed and connect it to the unit via a USB cable,
you can control DJ software via the unit.
• For details on the unit’s MIDI messages, visit the URL below.

     alphatheta.com/support/
• MIDI timing clock is always sent.
