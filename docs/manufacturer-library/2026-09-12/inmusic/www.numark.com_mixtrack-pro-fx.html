Mixing Starts Here

The Mixtrack Pro FX is a Serato DJ controller designed to inspire, develop and hone your DJ skills. It has everything you need to sound professional from the get-go: large 6-inch jog wheels, a 24-bit digital audio interface for pristine sound quality, six dedicated software effects buttons for easy transitions, 16 performance pads for hot cues, auto loop, fader cuts and sampling. Plus, you can even stream the music you love direct from TIDAL or SoundCloud via Serato DJ Lite! With Mixtrack Pro FX, everything the modern DJ demands is here - entering a world of pro DJ performance has never been simpler. Mixing Starts Here!

 

Mix with Ease—Instantly!

Center stage on Mixtrack Pro FX are two all-new premium paddle triggers for effortless command over Serato’s built-in software effects; there’s even quick access buttons to essential effects including Echo, Reverb, Flanger, Phaser, HPF (High Pass Filter) and LPF (Low Pass Filter) - a sure-fire guarantee that everything you need for creating build-ups, crafting unforgettable transitions and adding your own unique spin to your mix is only a touch away!

 

Command the Mix with Large Jog Wheels

You demand complete control over your mix, period. Mixtrack Pro FX’s large 6-inch capacitive-touch jog wheels emulate an authentic turntable feel for scratching and precise cue placement, while the rugged grooves on the platters side enables you to nudge your mix perfectly into place.

 

Spice Up Your Mix

Get creative, remix and perform unforgettable mixes with Mixtrack Pro FX’s 16 multi-function backlit performance pads! With dedicated quick-access pad mode buttons per deck for Hot Cues, Auto Loop, Sample and Fader Cuts (a signature crossfader effect that emulates a 1-4 click flare on the pads), reimagine and reinterpret your favourite tracks, creating something totally unique!

 

Dedicated Auto Loop

Mixtrack Pro FX’s Auto Loop makes it simple to mix and blend between songs, increasing or decreasing the loop segment with the on-board Loop Multiplier buttons to make electrifying build-ups.

Serato DJ Ready

We know your set demands the best, that’s why Mixtrack Pro FX comes pre-mapped with Serato DJ Lite for Mac & PC (software included) and is upgrade-ready to the full version of Serato DJ. For first-time DJs, Serato DJ Lite now comes with demo music and an easy on-boarding tutorial that will walk you through the basics so you can start DJing right away. Built on Serato DJ Pro technology, Serato DJ Lite is designed to be intuitive, reliable & feature rich.

 

Stream Millions of Songs

Never run out of music and keep the dance floor shaking! The Mixtrack Platinum FX comes with Serato DJ Lite, giving you access to stream millions of songs via WIFI from TIDAL or SoundCloud. Upgrade to Serato DJ Pro to unlock even more features.

 

Feature Packed

As if all of this wasn’t enough, Mixtack Pro FX features everything you need for immediate DJ performance, right out the box! Fusing plug and play high speed USB connectivity, a dedicated mixer section outfitted with a full 3-band EQ, filter knobs, channel faders and a cross fader, plus an onboard microphone input, RCA outputs and a headphone output, Mixtrack Pro FX covers all your bases, catering for anything your next DJ set demands!

Microphone not included.

SoundSwitch Premium Included

Simplify your lighting control with a 2-month trial of SoundSwitch premium DJ lighting software included with the Mixtrack Pro FX. SoundSwitch is a Digital Lighting Workstation that lets users create lighting displays that are performed in time with audio files when played back live in Serato DJ. It allows DJs and lighting designers the ability to sync lighting visuals with live audio seamlessly.
