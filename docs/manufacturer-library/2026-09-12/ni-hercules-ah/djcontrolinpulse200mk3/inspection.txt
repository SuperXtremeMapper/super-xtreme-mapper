  4. Jog wheel




Jog wheel: lets you move within the track, modify the
track’s playback, and scratch.


When the Beatmatch Guide function is enabled, the
BEAT ALIGN visual guides indicate the direction in
which you need to turn the ring (i.e. the edge) of the jog
wheel in order to align the beat grids of the two tracks.
When the two red arrows are not lit up, the beat grids
are aligned.


For more information on the Beatmatch Guide function,
please read the Beatmatch Guide section.

  5. Pads and modes




Mode buttons: let you enable up to four modes.
To enable a main mode, press HOT CUE or FX.
To enable a secondary mode (LOOP and SAMPLER),
simultaneously press SHIFT and HOT CUE or SHIFT
and FX. The mode button flashes.


The modes enabled on the pads vary depending on the
software being used. For more information about the
different modes with DJUCED, Serato DJ Lite and
Serato DJ Pro, please read the Modes and pads
section.


Performance pads: a pad performs an action.

     7.     Compatibility

USB Audio / USB MIDI
This controller is Plug & Play and can work without
drivers on any PC or Mac, as it complies with the USB
Audio and USB MIDI standards.



ASIO and the Windows® control panel
To use the DJControl Inpulse 200 MK3 controller with
Serato DJ Lite, you must install the ASIO drivers. The
ASIO drivers are available here:
https://support.hercules.com/product/djcontrolinpul
se200mk3/.


If you are using DJUCED, you can also download the
ASIO drivers in order to take advantage of lower latency
on your PC.


The ASIO drivers come with a control panel accessible
in    the   taskbar   on   PC,   or   in    the   C:\Program
Files\DJHERCULESMIX\Audio\DJ               Console   Series\
directory. This panel lets you adjust the ASIO buffer size,
and thus the latency.
