Ultra-compact DJ mixer controller
Z1 MK2 is the Traktor mix controller and soundcard that you can take anywhere and connect to any sound system.
Getting started with Traktor Z1 MK2
EQ and stem controls
Shape the high, medium, and low frequencies in your tracks, or tweak the volume on the drums, bass, instruments, and vocal stems.
Mixer FX controls
Apply powerful effects like Reverb, Dual Delay, Noise, Time Gater, and more.
VU meters
Always keep an eye on your levels.
Line faders and crossfader
High-quality faders allow for smooth blends and transitions.
Advanced displays
Three OLED displays show essential mixing and effect information.
Get notified
A light-based system lets you know when a track is about to end or if it’s running in a loop.
4 Channel Control
The Z1 MK2 can toggle between all 4 Traktor decks with the press of a button making this an ultra compact 4 channel mixer controller.
Prelisten Controls
Control the volume and mix between on air and prelisten signal using dedicated knobs to adjust the signal in your headphones.
Mixer Effects
Direct access to 5 Mixer Effect presets and chunky performance controls to bring effects into your mix.
Mix big, go small
Z1 has all the basic controls you need to get hands on with Traktor Pro 4 software across four decks.
EQs and stems
Tweak your sound with 3-band EQ and adjust individual stem volume for drums, bass, instruments, and vocals.
Pre-listen
Pre-listen to tracks before you mix them, adjust the cue/master balance, and regulate headphone volume.
Channel faders
Mix effortlessly with channel faders and a crossfader. The easy-to-read level meters provide instant visual feedback.
Mixer FX section
Bring some magic to your sets with intuitive control of nine different effects, including filter, reverb, delay, gater, and more.
Traktor Pro 4 included
Z1 ships alongside the latest version of our flagship DJ software – including flexible beatgrids, high-quality stem separation, iZotope Ozone Maximizer, and Pattern Player. Plus, there’s the studio-grade effects, reliable sync, and club-ready sound. Wherever you are and whatever you’re playing, Traktor Pro 4 gives you the freedom and flexibility to mix your own way.

Stem control
Adjust individual stem volume alongside Traktor Pro 4’s stem separation feature that allows precise isolation of drums, bass, instruments, and vocals.
Three OLED displays
Spend less time looking at your laptop with OLED displays that show essential mixing information, EQ and stem parameters, Mixer FX selection, and master output meter.
Light guide
Z1’s light system keeps you notified with key information, like when a track is about to run out or if it’s running in a loop. Plus, you can make it yours with customizable ambient lighting.
Mixer FX control
Get intuitive, one-knob control of nine effects that can be individually added to each channel: Filter, Reverb, Dual Delay, Noise, Time Gater, Flanger, Crush, Dotted Delay, and Barber Pole.
4-deck channel control
Z1 is small but powerful, offering dual-layer functionality for seamless control of up to four channels.
High-quality audio interface
Z1 lets you get hands-on with Traktor Pro 4 without the need for any extra gear, thanks to the integrated 24-bit/96 kHz audio interface. Connect a wide range of speakers with a mini-jack connector and a USB-B port to hook up your laptop.

Expand your setup
Traktor’s modular gear lets you add to your setup any way you need it. When it’s time to grow you can add more controllers, like X1 or F1, for full control of loops, cue points, effects, and Remix Decks.
Easy customization
Z1 can be quickly set up to meet your specific needs. Making adjustments to mapping is easy on Traktor Pro 4’s customization page. Plus, you can color-code each button group to quickly identify different functions.
Setup examples
Z1 gives you control over EQs, filters, track levels, and effects so you can prepare your set on the go.
Mix anywhere with full control over two decks and professional-quality sound in a compact and portable format.
Get a setup that’s both compact and versatile for pro DJs – combine Z1 MK2 and X1 MK3 for seamless mixing and advanced effects control with precise cueing, looping, and track navigation.
Combine one Z1 MK2 with two X1 MK3 controllers for full control over four decks. The Z1 manages mixing while the X1s offer precise control over individual deck cueing and effects.
