Creative 2-channel DJ controller
Break new ground with everything you need to start DJing and stand out – in a setup that can grow with you.
Start DJing with MX2
1
2
3
4
5

Two channel mixer
Club standard layout for full creative control over track transitions and blends.
High-performance pads
16 color-customisable pads add even more expression and flexibility. Switch between four physical modes: Stems, Pattern Player. Hot Cues, and Loops.
Jog wheels
Highly sensitive, non-slip, and made for the rewind. Nudge your tracks when mixing manually, cue up the next drop, or wheel it when things get heated.
Deck FX controls
Take your sets to another dimension with the tools to shape individual effects or layer them for a whole new sound.
Find your next track, fast
Dedicated controls for pre-listening, browsing favorites, adding tracks to a prep list, and easily switching to full-screen browse view.
Get notified
A light-based system lets you know when a track is about to end or if it’s running in a loop.
Built-in audio interface
A sound card with main out RCA and mini jack, headphone out mini jack and ¼ inch, mic input ¼ inch, and USB-C connector.
Mixer FX controls
Bring some magic to your sets with dedicated control of nine different effects, including filter, reverb, delay, gater, and more.
Pattern Player
Get physical with the built-in drum machine. Trigger synced rhythmic patterns using different percussion kits and add grooves, fills, and textures, on top of your mix.
Start out to stand out
From late-night solo sessions to the packed out club, MX2 has what you need for sets that set you apart.
Get up to speed
Start with all the DJ mainstays including channel faders, volume meters, a crossfader, and 3-band EQ.
Instinctive mixing
Get stuck in with a intuitive, highly visual layout that just makes sense. You’ll get to know the controls and sharpen your skills in no time.
Creative tools
Take your sets in a direction that no one saw coming, with tactile tools designed for experimentation.
Play anywhere
A powerful controller that’s portable enough to take everywhere – with all the connections you’ll need.

Traktor Pro 4 included
MX2 gets you hands-on with the full version our professional DJ software worth , including beat matching, looping, stems and powerful multi-effect chains. See the light
MX2 is highly visual. Find your way round no matter how dark the party with completely customizable control colors and a light-based system that lets you know when a track is about to run out or if it’s in loop mode.
Keep it moving
From streaming to street parties, get all the tools you need to bring the heat and keep the crowd moving,
Mixer FX
For the moments that matter, dedicated dials let you instantly apply filters or effects to individual channels. Drive the dance floor forward with drops, buildups, and smooth transitions, or switch it up by reaching for iconic Traktor reverb, creative delays, and more.
Deck FX
Push things further and break the mold with deep effects control that lets you twist, tweak, and transform your sound in real time. Choose from over 40 effects, layer them up and take your tracks into new territory with reverb, time-stretching algorithms, creative delays, and more.
Jog wheels
Nudge, scratch, and scroll. Jog wheels are ideal for beatmatching tracks, finding your starting position, or spinning it back for rewinds. And they’re made to match your spinning style with Turntable and Jog modes.
Find tracks faster
Get what you need in the heat of the moment with dedicated controls for pre-listening, browsing favorites, adding tracks to a prep list, and easily switching to full-screen browse view.
Push things further
16 customizable pads get you tactile with Traktor’s best features – for the most fun you can have in the booth.
Hotcues
This is your launchpad – add drops, trigger breakdowns, or rewind to that peak moment in an instant. Hotcues let you jump to key points in a track so you take the crowd on a journey.
Stems
Use the pads to blend parts from tracks you know to create tracks you don't. This mode works with Traktor Pro 4 stem separation that precisely splits tracks into drums, bass, instruments, and vocals.
Pattern Player
Go even deeper with Traktor’s drum kits. This mode transforms the pads into a step sequencer that you can program live to layer custom grooves into your set.
Loops
Keep your track going or build energy before a drop. The pads make it easy to create loops without interrupting the track flow and to jump right back in to where you left off.

Traktor MX2 FAQ
FAQs
Get answers to the most commonly asked questions here.
Does it come with DJ software included?
MX2 comes with the full version of
Traktor Pro 4
.
Can I use the Traktor MX2 without a computer?
No, the MX2 must connect via USB to a computer running
Traktor Pro 4
.
Can I use it without a power supply?
MX2 requires a power supply, which comes included in the box.
Is it compatible with Traktor Pro 3 or 4 software?
No – a full version of
Traktor Pro 4
is required but comes included free.
What are Mixer FX?
Mixer FX are simple but powerful one-dial effects for adding to your sets in the heat of the moment. They can all be assigned to the Filter dial on your MX2 controller. There are eight available effects, including Delay, Reverb, and Gater.
Does the MX2 include controls for Deck FX in Traktor Pro 4?
Yes, the MX2 offers full control over two of Traktor's powerful Deck FX units, with access to 40+ studio-quality effects that can be tweaked and layered for a totally new sound. Deck FX also offers control over the Pattern Player.
Are the jog wheels motorized?
No – they are the same size as Traktor S4’s jog wheels, but they are not motorized
Can I change the mapping?
Some parts of the mapping can be easily adjusted via the dedicated MX2 Preference Page.
Can I use the MX2 as a MIDI controller for other software?
Yes. The unit can be switched in and out of MIDI mode by pressing both SHIFT buttons at the same time. The LEDs will switch to blue to indicate MIDI mode.
Can I use MX2 alongside other Traktor controllers?
Yes – for example you could use it together with Traktor F1 to control clips in a Remix Deck.
What is the size difference between Traktor S2 MK3 and Traktor MX2?
Mk3: 486 × 59 × 265 mm
MX2: 511.4 × 61.9 × 323.1 mm
