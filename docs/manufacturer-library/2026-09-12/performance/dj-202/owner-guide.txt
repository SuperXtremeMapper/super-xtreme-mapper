 The top panel of the DJ-202 is divided into three sections by function: browser, deck, and mixer.
 Phones jack is located on the front side, and input/output jacks are located on the rear side.
 For details about each part, refer to the DJ-202 owner’s manual and the Serato DJ Intro manual.

    switching the unit on/off. However, this is normal and does not indicate a malfunction.
